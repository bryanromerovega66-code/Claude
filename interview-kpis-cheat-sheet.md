# Interview KPI Cheat Sheet — Sales Experience in Numbers

> Quick-reference numbers for today's interview. Not a script: glance at it when they ask "give me numbers".

---

## 1. Daily / Weekly Prospecting Funnel (both roles)

| Stage | Per day | Per week (5 days) | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outbound calls (dials) | **100** | **500** | — |
| Live conversations (connects) | **~20** | **~100** | **20% connect rate** (20 of 100) |
| Meetings booked | ~1 | **5** | **5% conversation-to-meeting** (5 of 100) · **1% dial-to-meeting** |
| Meetings advanced to next stage (qualified opps) | — | **2–3** | **40–60% meeting-to-opportunity** |
| Deals closed | — | **1 every 1–2 weeks** | **33–50% opp-to-close** · **10–20% meeting-to-close** |

**How to say it:**
- "I made around **100 dials a day** with a **20% connect rate**, so about **20 real conversations daily**."
- "That turned into **5 meetings a week**, and **40 to 60%** of those advanced to the next stage."
- "From there I closed **one deal every one to two weeks**, roughly a **10–20% meeting-to-close rate**."
- "Per closed deal I needed about **250–500 dials**, **50–100 conversations** and **5–10 meetings**. I knew my numbers, so I knew exactly how much activity I needed to hit quota."

## 2. Microsoft

- **Average deal size: $10,000+ USD** (full infrastructure implementation, not a single license).
- **Deals closed:** ~2–4 per month (1 every 1–2 weeks)

| Period | Deals | Revenue closed |
|---|---|---|
| Month | 2–4 | **$20K – $40K+** |
| Quarter | 6–12 | **$60K – $120K+** |
| Year | 24–48 | **$240K – $480K+** |

**How to say it:**
- "At Microsoft my average deal size was **$10K and up**, because we weren't selling a single product — we were implementing the **whole infrastructure** for the client."
- "Closing one deal every one to two weeks, that's roughly **$20K to $40K a month**, or **$240K to $480K a year** in closed revenue."
- "Bigger tickets meant more stakeholders (IT, finance, leadership), so multi-threading and a clear business case were key."

## 3. Alscore

- **Average deal size: $5,000 USD** (cost of implementing the technology).
- **Deals closed:** ~2–4 per month (1 every 1–2 weeks)

| Period | Deals | Revenue closed |
|---|---|---|
| Month | 2–4 | **$10K – $20K** |
| Quarter | 6–12 | **$30K – $60K** |
| Year | 24–48 | **$120K – $240K** |

**How to say it:**
- "At Alscore the average deal was about **$5K**, which was the implementation cost of the technology."
- "Same discipline on the funnel: **100 dials a day, 20% connect rate, 5 meetings a week, 1 close every one to two weeks** — around **$10K to $20K a month**."
